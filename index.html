FAQ

Questions we hear most often.

Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is happy to help.

Contact our team
Which industries do you recruit for?

We hire across 14+ sectors, including IT, banking and financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, retail and e-commerce, logistics, telecom, FMCG, construction, education, publishing and digital media.

What hiring models do you offer?

Permanent recruitment, contract and temporary staffing, bulk and specialised hiring, executive search and recruitment process outsourcing (RPO). We can combine them to suit your plan.

How do you screen candidates?

Every candidate is assessed for skills, experience and cultural fit against the brief we agree with you, so you only meet people who are relevant to the role.

Can recruiters or agencies partner with Talvex?

Yes. Our vendorship program is open to freelance recruiters, agencies and independent talent partners. Register your interest and we'll share mandates that match your expertise.

I'm looking for a job. How do I apply?

Browse our current openings on the Careers page, or submit your profile on the Recruitment page and we'll contact you when a matching role opens.
